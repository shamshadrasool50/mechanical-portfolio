About

I'm a Mechanical Engineering graduate from the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore, graduating in 2026. My interest sits at the intersection of manufacturing, maintenance and renewable energy — I like taking a system from a rough sketch through CAD, analysis and fabrication into something that actually turns, pumps or moves.

Recent industrial experience includes preventive and corrective maintenance at Alif Industry (Turkplast), where I supported troubleshooting of mechanical and pneumatic faults on production machinery, and an internship at Honda Atlas Cars Pakistan covering inventory handling, welding processes and quality inspection.

Outside coursework, I've built a hybrid vertical-axis wind turbine from blade design through wind-tunnel-style testing, an autonomous fire-fighting robot, a syringe-driven hydraulic robotic arm, and a solar water heater — each one taking a concept through design, fabrication and a working demo.
